# CLAUDE.md - Project Instructions

## Course Overview
AI for Research - A writingIO mini course on gathering, synthesizing, and verifying information with AI.

## Audience
- Professionals who research for work
- Students and academics
- Anyone needing to understand topics quickly
- People wanting more efficient research

## Tone and Style
- Practical and immediately applicable
- Balanced—acknowledges AI limitations
- Emphasizes verification alongside AI use

## Video Script Guidelines
- Brand name is "writingIO" (not "Writing IO" or "writing.io")
- Spell out numbers and acronyms for speech (e.g., "A I" not "AI")
- Scripts must be under 5000 characters for HeyGen
- First lesson includes Amanda intro and AI disclosure

## Key Themes
- AI helps at every stage of research
- Verification is essential, not optional
- Use AI for exploration, primary sources for confirmation
- Good research habits amplify AI's value
