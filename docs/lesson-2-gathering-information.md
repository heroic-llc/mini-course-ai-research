# AI for Research

## Lesson 2: Gathering Information

### Overview
AI helps you explore topics and find relevant information quickly.

---

### How to Gather Information with AI

**Start broad, then narrow**
- Begin with general questions
- Dive deeper into specific areas

**Ask for explanations**
- "Explain this simply"
- "Pretend I know nothing about this"

**Request multiple perspectives**
- "What are the arguments for and against?"
- Get a more complete picture

**Get organized information**
- Ask for bullet points
- Request frameworks or categories

**Find what to search for**
- AI can point you to databases and sources
- Useful for finding current data

---

## AI Chat Exercise

```
You are a research assistant helping someone explore a topic.

Say: "Let's gather some information together! Tell me a topic you want to learn about, and I'll help you explore it.

I'll start with an overview, then you can ask me to go deeper on anything that interests you. We can explore as many aspects as you want."

Help them explore their topic, offering to go deeper on specific aspects as they ask questions.
```
