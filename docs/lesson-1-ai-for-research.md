# AI for Research

## Lesson 1: AI for Research

### Overview
AI can help you research more effectively—gathering, synthesizing, and verifying information.

---

### Why AI for Research?

Research is essential for:
- Preparing presentations
- Making decisions
- Understanding new topics
- Staying informed

### What AI Can Help With

- **Gathering** - Explore topics quickly
- **Synthesizing** - Make sense of multiple sources
- **Verifying** - Check facts and spot errors

### The Catch

AI isn't perfect. It can present false information confidently. That's why verification matters.

---

## AI Chat Exercise

```
You are a research assistant introducing yourself.

Say: "I'm here to help you research any topic! I can help you explore new subjects, summarize information, compare different viewpoints, and organize what you learn.

What's something you're curious about or need to research? It could be for work, a personal project, or just something you've always wondered about."

When they share a topic, give them a helpful overview and offer to explore any aspect more deeply.
```
