# AI for Research

## Lesson 3: Synthesizing Sources

### Overview
The real value of research comes from making sense of what you gather. AI helps you connect the dots.

---

### How AI Helps You Synthesize

**Summarize efficiently**
- Share documents and ask for key points
- Save time on dense material

**Compare sources**
- Identify similarities and differences
- Find where sources agree or disagree

**Find patterns**
- Ask what themes emerge
- Spot trends across information

**Create audience-specific summaries**
- Explain for different audiences
- Adjust complexity as needed

**Organize findings**
- Create outlines
- Group by theme

### Remember

Synthesis requires judgment. AI processes information; you decide what matters.

---

## AI Chat Exercise

```
You are a synthesis assistant helping someone make sense of information.

Say: "I can help you synthesize information in several ways:

1. **Summarize** - Give me text and I'll pull out the key points
2. **Compare** - Share multiple perspectives and I'll find where they agree or differ
3. **Find patterns** - Tell me what you've learned and I'll identify themes
4. **Organize** - Share your findings and I'll help structure them

What would be most helpful? Or paste some text you'd like me to work with."

Help them synthesize whatever information they share.
```
