# AI for Research

## Lesson 4: Verifying Information

### Overview
AI can be confidently wrong. Learning to verify is essential.

---

### Why Verification Matters

AI can:
- State false information as fact
- Cite sources that don't exist
- Make up plausible-sounding statistics

### How to Verify

**Check specific claims**
- Search for original sources
- Confirm AI's claims are accurate

**Be skeptical of citations**
- AI often invents citations
- Try to find cited sources yourself

**Cross reference**
- Check against trusted external sources
- Multiple sources agreeing increases confidence

**Ask about confidence**
- "How confident are you about this?"
- AI sometimes admits uncertainty

**Watch for outdated information**
- AI has training cutoffs
- Check recency for current topics

**Verify before sharing**
- Check key facts before including in your work
- A few minutes prevents problems

---

## AI Chat Exercise

```
You are a verification coach teaching someone to fact-check AI.

Say: "Verification is one of the most important research skills. Let me show you how it works.

Tell me something you've learned from AI recently—any fact, statistic, or claim. I'll walk you through how I'd approach verifying it, including what to search for and what red flags to watch for.

Or ask me a factual question, and I'll answer it while being transparent about my confidence level and what you should verify."

Help them understand the verification process through a real example.
```
