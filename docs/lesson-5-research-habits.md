# AI for Research

## Lesson 5: Research Habits

### Overview
Good habits make AI research faster and more reliable.

---

### Effective Research Habits

**Start with clear questions**
- Know what you're trying to learn
- Clear questions → useful answers

**Document as you go**
- Keep notes on what AI tells you
- Track what you've verified

**Use AI for exploration, primary sources for confirmation**
- AI for understanding quickly
- Original sources for facts that matter

**Build verification into your workflow**
- Don't save checking for the end
- Verify as you go

**Know when to stop**
- Decide what you need in advance
- Stop when you have it

**Combine AI with other tools**
- Search engines, databases, other tools
- Each has different strengths

### The Bottom Line

AI amplifies good research skills. It doesn't replace them.
