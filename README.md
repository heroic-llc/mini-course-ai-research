# AI for Research

A writingIO mini course on using AI to gather, synthesize, and verify information.

## Course Overview

Learn how to research more effectively with AI—from exploring new topics to verifying what you learn.

## Lessons

1. **AI for Research** - Introduction and overview
2. **Gathering Information** - Using AI to explore and find information
3. **Synthesizing Sources** - Making sense of what you gather
4. **Verifying Information** - Fact-checking and avoiding AI errors
5. **Research Habits** - Building effective AI research practices

## Target Audience

- Professionals who research for work
- Students and academics
- Anyone who needs to understand new topics quickly
- People who want to research more efficiently
